Stump Grinding Service and Pricing in the Pensacola Area

What you get

We grind the stump and major surface roots below grade, rake grindings as agreed, and leave the area ready for what comes next. Keep the chips as mulch or ask about haul-off. Extension services describe a typical grind as 8 to 12 inches below ground so the spot can take new sod or plantings; tell us your plan for the hole and we grind to suit.

How pricing works

Diameter, root flare, access and stump count set the price. A single open-yard stump is usually in the $200 to $400 range. Tight gates, slopes and buried debris can change that. You get a free exact quote before any work starts.

Before we grind

Florida's Underground Facility Damage Prevention and Safety Act requires notice to Sunshine 811 at least two full business days before digging. Sunshine 811 names stump grinding, and the ticket is free. We call in locates where they apply so buried lines stay marked before the cutter goes in.

After the grind

Fresh chips are high in carbon and can tie up nitrogen if you plant right away. The spot settles as remaining roots rot, so a new tree belongs at least a few feet away from the old stump line. Rotting stumps and dead roots can also attract carpenter ants and give subterranean termites a path toward wood near the house; UF/IFAS advises keeping dead wood at least three feet from structures.

Storm stumps

After a hurricane or high wind, tree crews often leave root balls and snapped trunks. Hurricane Isaias made landfall near Destin on October 9, 2026, as a Category 2 storm with 105 mph winds, and the National Weather Service reported hundreds of trees snapped or uprooted in Escambia County with heavy damage across Santa Rosa County. We grind those leftovers once the tree work is done.

Who we help

Homeowners, landscapers, property managers and builders across Escambia County, Santa Rosa County and nearby counties. We also subcontract stump grinding for tree services that want the stump finished after a removal.

Stumps are not yard waste

City of Pensacola collection does not take tree stumps or trunks. Escambia County and City of Milton rules put debris removal on the contractor for tree work. Ask about haul-off when you request a quote if you do not want the chips left on site.
