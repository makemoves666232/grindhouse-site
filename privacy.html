Privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026. This page covers the quote form on stumpsrx.com, the advertising and analytics tags on this site, and the name, phone number, and job address you send through a StumpsRx Facebook form or Messenger chat.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote, we collect your name, phone number, email if you give one, the address of the job, and what you want done. That includes a stump left after a storm. We also keep photos of the work when you send them or when the crew takes them on the job.

How we use it

We use that information to price the job, schedule the crew, and do the work. We call or message you about that request. A marketing text goes out only if you check a separate consent box. Leaving the box unchecked does not stop you from getting a quote. Sending the form is not permission to text you.

Texts

If you opt in, StumpsRx may text the mobile number you gave us. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information. The people who answer the phone and run the crew can see it. A quote request sent from this site goes to our email and to our own job records. Facebook, Google, the texting service, and the form service see what they need to deliver the lead or the message, or to measure our ads as described below, under their own policies.

Ads, analytics and call tracking

This site uses Google Analytics, and Google Ads tags when we run ads. They set cookies and record which search or ad brought you here and whether you called or sent the quote form. If you reach the site from one of our ads, the link carries a click code from Google. We keep that code in your browser for up to 90 days and save it with your quote request, along with the first page you saw and the site that sent you, so we can tell which ad led to the request.

When you send the form, we may send Google a scrambled (hashed) copy of your phone number or email so Google can match your request to the ad you clicked. If you call a number shown in one of our Google ads, or on this site after clicking an ad, the call may pass through a Google forwarding number, and Google may record the call after telling you so at the start.

You can turn off ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com and Google Analytics with the browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
