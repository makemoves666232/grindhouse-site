Counties we cover

Rather than publish thin county clones, this hub explains coverage by county and links to the cities where most jobs land.

Escambia County, FL

Pensacola, Pensacola Beach and Perdido Key sit here. Expect live oak, pine and palm stumps, plus post-storm root balls near the Gulf and bayous.

Santa Rosa County, FL

Gulf Breeze, Milton, Pace and Navarre. Mix of peninsula yards, subdivision lots and rural parcels along the Blackwater and East Bay.

Okaloosa County, FL

Western Okaloosa within the 50-mile radius, including areas toward Mary Esther and Fort Walton when the job size makes the trip worthwhile. Call to confirm.

Walton County, FL

Western Walton edges of the radius for larger clearing or multi-stump work. Confirm distance when you request a quote.

Baldwin County, AL

Orange Beach, Gulf Shores edge and Foley-area jobs within range. Barrier-island access and HOA rules often apply.

Escambia County, AL
